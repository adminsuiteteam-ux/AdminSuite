1. Introduction

Welcome to Admin Suite ("we", "our", or "us"). Admin Suite is an all-in-one workforce management, enterprise communication, and operational administration platform designed for modern organizations, managers, and employees.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices concerning the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of information when you use our Android mobile application, web applications, and backend cloud services (collectively, the "Services").

By installing, accessing, or using Admin Suite, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies, please discontinue use of the Services immediately.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide workplace administration, team collaboration, and reliable security:

A. Personal Identification Data

  • Account Profiles: Your name, business email address, phone number (optional), and encrypted password hash.
  • Google Identity Information: When authenticating via "Sign in with Google", we receive your verified Google email address, full name, Google user ID, and avatar image.

B. Workplace & Employment Information

  • Organization Credentials: Company name, branch location, assigned department, and workspace role (Admin, Manager, Employee).
  • Workforce Tracking: Employee ID, job title, assigned shift schedules, clock-in and clock-out timestamps, and leave requests.
  • Administrative Payroll Data: Salary grades, payslip summaries, and reimbursement requests managed by your workspace administrators.
  • Task & Project Management: Tasks, due dates, task status transitions, and workplace priorities.

C. Communications & Real-Time Calling

  • Workspace Messaging: Direct chats, group channels, messages, timestamps, and attachment media.
  • Real-Time Voice and Video Calls: Real-time voice and video streams enabled via Daily.co WebRTC infrastructure.

🔒 Zero Call Recording Guarantee: Admin Suite never records, stores, or transcribes your voice or video calls on any server. All audio and video streams are transmitted transiently in real time between participants.

D. Technical & Device Telemetry

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, app build, and system language.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Expo push tokens used to ring your device on incoming calls and deliver timely alerts.
  • Diagnostic Crash Logs: Anonymized stack traces gathered via Sentry for application stability and bug resolution.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

Admin Suite requests the following Android permissions strictly to power core workspace capabilities:

Permission System Identifier Operational Purpose
Microphone RECORD_AUDIO Transmits your voice during real-time peer-to-peer and team voice/video calls.
Camera CAMERA Transmits video during live video conferences and allows taking photos for profile avatars or task attachments.
Audio Settings MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Manages audio routing between phone earpiece, speakerphone, and Bluetooth headsets during calls.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays incoming call alerts, direct chat messages, task assignments, and workspace alerts.
Vibration & Wake Lock VIBRATE / WAKE_LOCK Rings and vibrates your device like a standard telephone call and illuminates the screen to answer incoming calls.
Boot Completed RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reinitializes notification listeners after your phone restarts so you don't miss calls or urgent tasks.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To administer your organization's workspace, verify user roles, and track attendance.
  • To ring and connect real-time voice and video calls between workspace colleagues.
  • To authenticate accounts securely and protect against unauthorized access.
  • To dispatch high-priority push notifications for messages and incoming calls.
  • To diagnose crashes, improve system stability, and optimize network performance.

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal or workspace data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with vetted service providers to support our platform infrastructure:

  • Google Identity & Google Play: Authentication services and app distribution. (Privacy Policy)
  • Daily.co (Daily Inc.): Real-time WebRTC audio and video streaming. (Privacy Policy)
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging: Reliable push notification delivery. (Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry: Application crash tracking and diagnostic monitoring. (Privacy Policy)

6. Data Security

We enforce rigorous security standards across all layers of our application:

  • End-to-End Transport Security: All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Secure Token Storage: Authentication tokens are stored using hardware-backed cryptographic keystores (expo-secure-store).
  • Tenant Isolation: Strict database row-level security ensures no workspace can access another organization's records.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and international privacy regulations (GDPR / CCPA), every user has the right to request deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion:

  1. In-App: Go to Settings > Help & Support > Request Account Deletion in the Admin Suite mobile app.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your registered address to privacy@adminsuite.com with the subject line "Delete Account & Data".

Upon request verification, your profile, authentication credentials, messages, and associated personal records will be permanently purged or irreversibly anonymized from our production databases within thirty (30) days.

8. Children's Privacy

Admin Suite is strictly an enterprise workforce tool intended for professional business use by adult individuals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, rectify, port, or request deletion of your personal information, as well as the right to restrict or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@adminsuite.com.
